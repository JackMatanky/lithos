# Story 6.6: Epic 6 Retrospective - Technical Debt Refactoring

## Status

ready-for-dev

## Story

**As a** development team,
**I want** to reflect on Epic 6 refactoring patterns and outcomes,
**so that** we can apply lessons learned to future development and maintain code quality.

## Context

Epic 6 focused on decomposing god-objects into event-driven components following SOLID principles. This retrospective captures:
- What worked well
- What could be improved
- Patterns to reuse
- Metrics showing improvement
- Lessons for future refactoring
