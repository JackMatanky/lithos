# Test Design: Story 3.30 (Event-Driven Architecture)

Date: 2025-11-20
Designer: Quinn (Test Architect)

## Test Strategy Overview

- Total test scenarios: 10
- Unit tests: 4 (40%)
- Integration tests: 5 (50%)
- E2E tests: 1 (10%)
- Priority distribution: P0: 9, P1: 1, P2: 0

## Test Scenarios by Acceptance Criteria

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| ID | Level | Priority | Test | Justification |

| ID | Level | Priority | Test | Justification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Risk Coverage

- Coupling regressions mitigated via 3.30-E2E-101.
- EventBus reliability ensured by 3.30-UNIT-103/105 and 3.30-INT-103.
- Performance risk handled via 3.30-INT-104.

## Recommended Execution Order

1. 3.30-UNIT-101 through 3.30-UNIT-105 (bus + event contracts)
2. 3.30-INT-101, 3.30-INT-102 (publisher/subscriber wiring)
3. 3.30-E2E-101 (CLI validation)
4. 3.30-INT-103, 3.30-INT-104 (integration + performance)
