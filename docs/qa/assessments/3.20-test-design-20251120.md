## Risk Coverage

No dedicated risk profile exists; scenarios 3.20-INT-004/005/006 mitigate the highest risks (transactional corruption, performance regressions, concurrency issues).

## Recommended Execution Order

1. 3.20-UNIT-001, 3.20-UNIT-002 (fast fail)
2. 3.20-INT-001 through 3.20-INT-004 (structural + transactional)
3. 3.20-INT-005/006 (performance + concurrency)
4. 3.20-INT-007 (docs)
5. 3.20-E2E-001 (final gate)
